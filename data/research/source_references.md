## App-contract source IDs

Every `source_id` in `analog_features.csv` and `site_evidence.json` is a row of `dataset_catalog.csv` (TerraAnalog `dataset_id`s, plus `TA-ENGINE` for engine-derived values and `TA-PF-LOGISTIC` for the in-house permafrost fallback).
Body radii come from the DEM products themselves (LOLA PDS label; MOLA mosaic GeoTIFF CRS). Reference diameters are given only where a fetched Wikipedia revision states them (Shackleton, Jezero, Gale, Arsia Mons); all others are null.
